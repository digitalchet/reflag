How to use Reflag

Find flags quickly

The search field is ready when this page opens. You can also start typing after clicking a flag or region heading, and your typing will move into search automatically.

Search by country name or two-letter code. Separate several searches with commas:

cn, ru, it, sp, de, fr

Selecting a result removes its search term while leaving the others ready. A single completed search clears automatically.

Choose individually or by region

Press any flag to toggle it. Expand a continental section to browse its flags or use its All button for the whole region. The switch beside Selected flags enables or disables the complete collection.

Website access

The extension works on HTTP and HTTPS websites by default. Turn on the Selected websites only switch to reveal the domain list and restrict Reflag to those websites. Favicons are read from the browser when available; no external icon service is contacted.

Privacy and text integrity

Everything runs locally. The extension does not track browsing or send page content anywhere. It preserves the original Unicode for copying, search, and accessibility.
